# Dashboard brand kit, draft 02

24 October 2026. Mapbox-inspired revision of the local Tok River website. This
records the current proposal; it is not a final naming or logo decision.

## Reference and adaptation

Amit selected [Mapbox](https://www.mapbox.com/) after rejecting the original
near-black land view and sparse UI treatment. Borrow its confident geometric
type, blue actions, rounded controls and spacious hierarchy. The user's dark
theme remains binding. Use our PROJECT placeholder and contour motif.

The observed reference uses Cera Pro and a white ground. Our implementation uses
open DM Sans and deep navy instead. See the distinction between extracted facts
and adaptations in [the reference study](../../research/mapbox-reference/DESIGN.md).

checks apply to those flat colour pairs, not a blanket accessibility claim.

DM Sans 400/500/600/700 carries the UI; Plex Mono 400/500 carries selected
measurement and record values. Fonts are self-hosted Latin subsets. Desktop
title is 42 px/700. Labels are deliberately more readable than the first draft.

## Spatial language

- Full-width rounded terrain workspace, 20 px outer radius.
- Floating 330 px evidence inspector on desktop, with a visible scroll cue for
  full detail. At 1120 px and below it joins the normal flow beneath the map.
- Compact blue filter pills and a working records CTA.
- Rounded timeline and records surfaces, calm separators and more generous
  spacing. Preserve source links, qualification notes and pending ownership.

## Land treatment

Use actual Tok River geography, styled in visible olive/sage greens, blue water
and shaded surface relief. Current source roles are OpenFreeMap Liberty vector
context and Mapzen Terrarium DEMs, with separate terrain/hillshade sources.
Initial pitch is 48°, bearing −12°, with 1.35× visual elevation exaggeration.
The elevation control can switch terrain rendering off and on.

This carries the earlier draft's depth treatment without copying its invented
terrain shape. Green is a cartographic design treatment, not a vegetation or
mineral probability. Do not introduce generated ore bodies, fake contours,
parcel polygons or heatmaps.

## Evidence boundaries

All 67 sample positions and original measurements remain authoritative. Primary
gold, separate retest and secondary lithium remain distinct. The timeline stays
month precision. Arjin, Muhd and Salman retain their pending workstreams. No
probability, score or recommendation is added to fill the redesigned panels.

Root `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` document the implemented system.
The original brand board remains a historical draft, not the current website's
token source.
