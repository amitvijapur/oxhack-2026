# Eastern Interior Alaska contract observations

Research checked 25 October 2026. These five historical observations are classified in [regional-comparables.csv](regional-comparables.csv). They are not a current regional average, offer, or estimate of short-term assignment proceeds. Blank numeric fields mean not separately disclosed, not zero.

The strongest disclosed **signing-payment** example is Tibbs. Northway also paid a separate US$25,000 scholarship in its initial year, so its US$30,000 lease cash is not its full entry-year cash requirement. The directly **Tok-labeled** example is the Peak Gold JV claim option, but its observed amount is an exercise payment in a related-party transaction. Northway supplies another initial lease payment in the eastern Interior, with substantial additional obligations. Tanacross supplies a nearby large earn-in comparison. LMS is acquisition context only. Exact distance from Orecast's particular Tok River parcel was not established, so this is not a geographic nearest-neighbor valuation.

## Primary evidence and audit points

### Tanacross

[Kenorland announcement, 20 July 2022](https://www.kenorlandminerals.com/news/kenorland-minerals-options-the-tanacross-project-to-antofagasta/) establishes the original footprint and announced terms. [2023 Q2 financial statements, note 6, printed p22](https://fr.kenorlandminerals.com/_resources/financials/2023-Q2-FS.pdf?v=121110) explicitly spread the cash over eight years. This disproves the local description of US$1m as upfront cash. [Termination announcement, 28 March 2024](https://www.kenorlandminerals.com/news/2024/kenorland-minerals-announces-termination-of-tanacross-project-earn-in-agreement-with-antofagasta-plc-and-highlights-exploration-upside-at-south-taurus/) establishes the outcome. The received amount in the CSV is dated to the Q2 disclosure, not asserted to be lifetime final cash.

### Tibbs

[Tectonic 2019 Q2 MD&A, dated 10 September 2019, printed p4](https://www.tectonicmetals.com/_resources/financials/MDA-Q2-2019-FINAL.pdf?v=070606) separates signing cash, anniversary cash, work, royalty and contingent production payments. [2025 year-end MD&A, printed pp6–7](https://www.tectonicmetals.com/_resources/financials/MDA-Tectonic-2025-Q4.pdf?v=040401) confirms subsequent payment status and satisfied exploration commitment. The CAD figure beside each explicitly identified USD payment is a reporting translation, not another cash obligation.

### Northway

[Tectonic audited 2018 statements, note 6, printed p17](https://www.tectonicmetals.com/_resources/financials/FS-Tectonic-2018YE-AUDITED.pdf?v=090403) separate initial lease cash, staged payments, work and scholarship obligations. [2021 Q3 MD&A, dated 22 November 2021, Northway section](https://www.tectonicmetals.com/_resources/financials/MDA-Tectonic-2021-Q3-vSEDAR.pdf?v=100104) establishes amendment and termination. CSV annual terms describe the original agreement, not an assumed surviving 2021 schedule.

### Tok state claims

[Contango FY2021 Form 10-K, Properties section and accounting note](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1502377/000143774921021206/conta20210630b_10k.htm) establishes the option date, approximate footprint and completed exercise. Signing the option and buying the claims are separate events. The company's ownership of part of the purchasing JV makes this an unsuitable stand-alone arm's-length market benchmark.

### LMS

[Gold Reserve 2016 Annual Information Form, filed 28 April 2017, printed p20; currency convention p7](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1072725/000107272517000012/gdrzfform40fexhibit991042817.htm) establishes a completed acquisition and retained royalties. [Corvus announcement, 13 January 2016, SEC exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1507964/000127956916002539/ex991.htm) identifies the cash component while closing was pending; the later Gold Reserve filing establishes closing. Its acquisition consideration is not a lease premium.

## Transferability and resale evidence

None of the reviewed summaries establishes that the purchased option or lease can be assigned freely. An underlying asset changing hands on exercise does not establish transferable option rights. No public 30-, 90- or 365-day assignment prices, assignment success rates, or comparable resale series was found in this bounded review. Obtain the executed contract's assignment/consent provisions and actual local quotes before using those quantities as measured inputs. This is an evidence gap, not proof that no assignment market exists.

No average, currency conversion, inflation adjustment or per-acre normalization is calculated. Exploration work, scholarship obligations, royalties, success payments, initial premiums and asset acquisition prices remain distinct. These documents do not by themselves validate Orecast's assumed resale margin.
