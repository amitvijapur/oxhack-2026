## Result for entry pricing

**The existing collection cannot support an average current Tok option premium or an expected assignment margin.** Its ten rows combine project acquisitions, company/share transactions, a related-party option exercise, a terminated earn-in and an antimony option. None is flagged as a verified available Tok assignment. There is no matched entry/assignment-exit cash ledger, observed resale spread or assignment time-to-sale in these rows. This is absence in the reviewed collection, not a claim that no such transactions exist.

The best leads for further verification are Tanacross for historical regional option structure, Peak Gold for a historical Tok-area exercise, and Delta for nearby project-package context. Each has a different price definition and substantial exclusions. Do not pool their headline amounts or divide them by Area A/B sample counts or bounding-box acreage.

**Material source correction.** The local Tanacross row says “US$1m cash up front.” The parent lane's fresh primary-source review contradicts that description. Kenorland's original announcement describes aggregate US$1m cash payments, and its 2023 year-end financials describe US$1m over eight years with US$100,000 received. The local US$1m must therefore be quarantined as a conflicted upfront-premium label. The US$100,000 is a reported receipt, not automatically a day-zero payment or a complete assignment price. The primary review is being completed by the parent lane; this lane did not fetch the documents. ## Excluded benchmarks and dimensional checks

The local surface-sale series is explicitly state **surface parcels**, with changing parcel mix. In 2025, statewide median paid price was US$6,885.76/acre for 109 parcels. The nearby Richardson/Alaska Highways grouping had one sale at US$8,503.40/acre. Neither is a mineral-interest or option-premium mean. A one-observation regional median cannot ground a general Tok premium. No surface price floor is justified unless a specified interest actually conveys surface rights and supports that valuation separately.

Annual claim rent, annual labour and required exploration work are obligations with their own payer, timing and tenure. They are not purchase consideration or assignment-sale prices. The old source README's 2000 rent schedule is superseded by the newer dated cost report within the bundle; neither establishes current legal applicability or which party owes a cost for a proposed contract.

Area A and Area B contain 33 and 34 measured sample points. They are **comparison partitions, not legal parcels or defined investable interests**. Search windows are sample bounding boxes. Active-claim point overlaps are 2/33 for A and 20/34 for B; the 17 Contango overlaps in B identify recorded holders, not current offers. Multiple records can overlap one sample, so row counts and summed claim acres can double count. A claim-count multiplier, sample count, bounding-box area or 13 Delta overlaps cannot allocate a transaction price to either area.

Dimensionally, `(USD/package) / (acres/package)` gives a descriptive USD/acre only for a reconciled package area and consideration. Multiplying that ratio by an independently specified parcel area would still require comparable rights, fraction, stage, commodity, date, tenure and obligations. Here the second parcel interest and its compatible acreage are missing. `sediment ppm`, model-assumed `recoverable ounces`, claims and sample points are not interchangeable acreage or reserve denominators. Converting ha to acres fixes units only; it does not fix comparability.

No annual growth, dollar return or profit margin can be inferred just by averaging these headline prices. A proposed contract margin needs an identified interest and dated entry payments, obligations, exercise/assignment permissions and a separately supported exit price or bid for that same interest. The model's US$650 premium and US$50 entry fee remain synthetic assumptions; this audit supplies no evidence to relabel US$700 upfront risk as a measured regional average.

## Useful next normalization

Keep one agreement and each dated payment component separately. Required fields are property/geometry and interest ID; ownership fraction; commodity and stage; original currency; announced versus paid amount; payment date and condition; cash versus shares/warrants; royalties; work and carry obligations; expiry; exercise consideration; assignment permission; counterparties and related-party status; source publication date; termination/completion/amendment status; and a separate observed exit ledger if present. Missing fields stay unknown.

Two prior Nevada examples in `research/strategy-amendments/contract-valuation-research.md` support this component schema, not a Tok price. Trench separates US$650,000 option payments, shares, US$500,000 exploration and retained royalties. Legal Tender separates US$200,000 staged payments, drilling metres and royalties/resource payments. Neither is among these ten Alaska rows or suitable for silently widening the Tok average.
