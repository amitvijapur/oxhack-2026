# OxHack opening ceremony and initial brainstorm

Source: Granola notes pasted by Amit on 24 October 2026. These are the working event brief. Sponsor spellings, precise deadlines, and terms have not been independently verified. Discord research was cancelled by Amit.

## Event

- Approximately 350 attendees at a 25-hour hackathon, including the extra hour from the clock change.
- Hosted under Oxford University, with recurring Oxford hackathons being restarted after earlier events including OxHack 2014 and Oxford Hacks 2018.
- Theme: **Time**, which must be incorporated into every project.

## Sponsors and challenges

### HRT, platinum sponsor

- Best use of data for predictions and decision-making.
- Open-ended; financial markets are not required.
- Reported prize: a £1,000 voucher per person on the winning team, plus lunch with engineers in the London office.
- Roles mentioned: AlgoDev, Software Engineer, and a new Data Scientist internship for next summer.

### Zed / Delta

- Best project built collaboratively using Delta, described in the notes as Zed's new coding agent and messaging tool.
- At least two team members must work in Delta.
- Judged on speed, real-world utility, and clean code.
- Free tokens were mentioned. URL supplied in the notes: https://delta.dev.
- Simultaneous multi-user development was discussed. Product capabilities and setup remain unverified.

### MLH

- Multiple API prizes, with Gemini, a Streamdeck for best use of “Tart data,” Blockchain, and a “Dev voicing” challenge mentioned.
- The quoted sponsor/challenge names may be transcription errors and need clarification if targeted.

### Handmade prize, sponsored by Citadel

## Initial project direction

- Explore unconventional or social data, such as in-app purchases or game skin sales, as signals for market or commodity movements.
- Identify an emotional or cultural connection to a commodity, model it, and generate predictions.
- A gamified or playful presentation is possible, but the prediction and decision value should be demonstrable.
- Considering Delta for collaborative coding and a shared repository with an agentic coding harness.
- HRT and Delta are potential targets, not confirmed team choices.
