## Opening

Orecast investigates how geological evidence, changing scenarios and the time
available to find a buyer affect a mineral decision. It makes the forecast
assumptions and the resulting option calculation inspectable.

## Explaining the model

The new order is support contrast, assumed gold forecast, material value,
mineral-interest value, assignment proceeds, sale/no-sale cash and percentage
return. The response coefficient changes gold first. It does not directly mark
up assignment proceeds. Revaluation happens at every possible sale date.

At zero assumed growth, the baseline 30/90/180-day cash outputs remain
-$410.00, +$7.60 and +$369.89. Dividing by the same $700 upfront risk gives
-58.57%, +1.09% and +52.84%. These are non-annualized expectations under an
assumed sale hazard, not realized or forecast-validated returns.

The 90-day sale probability is 48.8%, while 51.2% of paths do not sell. The
no-sale cash branch is -$700 and the median cash outcome is also -$700.
An unsold mark is separate from cash. The horizon and 15-day closing buffer
must both fit inside the assumed 365-day maturity.

## Questions and validation

**Has Orecast demonstrated a predictive edge?** No. The small retrospective
equity pilot tested a separate event adjustment, not the Tok option calculation
or swarm lift. At five sessions, five held-out observations had MAE of 2.5562
percentage points for no change, 3.1291 for gold only and 8.0596 for gold plus
event. The event adjustment did not improve the baseline.

**Why does approval lower the result?** The recorded mining-support contrast is
negative against the matching sports-placebo reference. The calculation exposes
that sign. These are simulated reactions with failed research diagnostics, not
observed market-price effects or buyer demand.

**Does 7% cap losses?** No. It bounds an assumed gold-price response coefficient.
It does not cap capital loss or establish a calibrated price relationship.

**Do assays establish recoverable ounces or available rights?** No. Sediment
concentration supports the evidence screen. Quantity, geological success, contract
terms and liquidity remain separate assumptions.

Next validation needs dated real option prices, completed and unsold assignments,
and genuinely pre-outcome swarm predictions evaluated chronologically against
simple baselines. Preserve the negative pilot and unavailable swarm lift when editing.
