Reviewed source revision: [`37991ad5de416e5abf8541264c5f4926a2ec91e8`](https://github.com/amitvijapur/oxhack-2026/tree/37991ad5de416e5abf8541264c5f4926a2ec91e8).

Both show Area A, 90 days and simulated day 5. Approval displays −$29,344.55 expected cash and −97.65% expected option return; blockage displays −$29,339.78 and −97.64%. Both show SKIP. The workflow snapshot is hypothetical, with $30,050 upfront risk and assumed economics and liquidity. Recorded support does not clear failed research checks. The required-resale diagnostic is an underwriting hurdle, not a forecast or observed quote.

Screenshots retain the actual application identity shown on screen. They have not been retouched to imply a rename or alter model output. The approval image is used in slides 4 and 5; the blockage image supports rehearsal and provenance.

`explorer-land.png`, `terrain-crop.png` and `swarm-network.png` are reused actual explorer/geographic and research-view captures from the approved deck. Geography and support views do not supply executable rights or measured resale liquidity.
