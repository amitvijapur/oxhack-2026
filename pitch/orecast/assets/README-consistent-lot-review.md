# Consistent-lot review capture provenance

`workflow-consistent-lot-review-approval.jpg` and `workflow-consistent-lot-review-blockage.jpg` are actual 1280×720 local production captures of `/swarm`, Area A, 90 days and simulated day 5. At capture time the model input was local unpublished commit `69a0b5b4d2ca8db16cc46f15ff0b07f954e72ed2`. It is now published as [orecast-model-inputs-v1](https://github.com/amitvijapur/oxhack-2026/tree/orecast-model-inputs-v1); capture and artifact bytes are unchanged.

Approval shows $69,027.40 assignment, $3,610.97 expected cash and 12.02% expected return. Blockage shows $69,479.01, $3,831.36 and 12.75%. Both use one fixed synthetic exposure of 23,076.92308 assumed troy ounces and $184,615.38462 buyer consideration, with $30,050 upfront capital and fixed $50 entry/exit fees. These are hypothetical conditional candidates, not observed returns or trades. The captures retain the actual Orecast UI and disclose that the invented exposure is not measured resources or a valuation from historical payments.

The approval capture is used on slides 4/5; blockage supports rehearsal. `explorer-land.png`, `terrain-crop.png` and `swarm-network.png` are reused actual geographic/research captures from the approved style source. They do not supply available rights, recoverable resources or measured resale liquidity.

Imagery: **Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics**, through the [Esri World Imagery service](https://server.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer). Terrain: **Mapzen Terrarium**, through [AWS Open Data](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/). Geographic context is separate from the [Alaska DGGS sediment publication](https://doi.org/10.14509/29688). Preserve these credits when reusing images.
