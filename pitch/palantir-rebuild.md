# Orecast presentation layouts

Keep the approved Palantir Q2 2021 composition system with Orecast's identity.
Use white paper #fafafa, charcoal #0a0a0a and sparse signal green #22c55e.
DM Sans 400 carries editorial copy; DM Sans 300 carries focal metrics.

At 1280×720, editorial text begins near x42. Typical asymmetric dark panels use
x648/y16/w616/h688 with 24px corners. Section furniture is small and quiet.
The native Orecast folio is 11–12px metadata. Primary wordmarks have more space.

Reference page map: cover1; timeline12; evidence5; exploded layers11;
scenario imagery13; horizon chart20; full-image close23; cash table43;
historical appendix44. The reference guides composition, not business claims.
Do not copy Palantir's company identity or introduce its performance figures.

Keep the gold-first diagram, cash chart and tables editable. Avoid generic
card grids, oversized bold headers and repeated full-width footer rules.
Inspect every edited slide and compare its appearance with the supplied PDF.
