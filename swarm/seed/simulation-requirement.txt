Simulate how support for gold mining at Tok, Alaska, changes over 5 days among the 60 stakeholders in the cast: state and federal officials, people whose work depends on the Manh Choh mine and its ore haul, residents of Tok and the haul route, gold funds, and individual investors. On day 1 a local news wire reports that gold has broken out from about $4,000 to about $4,500 per ounce, and on day 3 it reports further local or market news. Track which groups react first, how far and in which direction their support moves, and how views spread between groups. Use this to predict what these stakeholders come to expect for the gold price, for Alaska's policy on mining near Tok (mine approvals such as the pending Redpoll Ridge Project, and mining tax), and for local political leaning for or against mining and the politicians who back it.
